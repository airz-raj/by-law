RENT AGREEMENT

This Rent Agreement is made at Example Nagar on the 1st day of October 2025

BETWEEN

Shri Sunil Bharti, son of Shri Om Prakash Bharti, resident of House 21, Lane 4, Ganga Vihar, Sector 00, Example Nagar (hereinafter called "the Landlord")

AND

Ms. Rehana Qureshi, daughter of Shri Abdul Qureshi, resident of Sector 00, Example Nagar (hereinafter called "the Tenant")

WHEREAS the Landlord is the owner of the second floor of House 21, Lane 4, Ganga Vihar, Sector 00, Example Nagar, consisting of two rooms, a kitchen, a bathroom and a balcony (hereinafter called "the said premises"), and has agreed to let the same to the Tenant on the terms below.

NOW THIS AGREEMENT WITNESSES AS FOLLOWS:

1. The tenancy shall commence on 1 October 2025 and shall subsist for a period of eleven months, renewable by mutual consent in writing.

2. The Tenant shall pay to the Landlord a monthly rent of Rs. 14,000/- (Rupees Fourteen Thousand only), payable on or before the seventh day of each calendar month.

3. The monthly rent stated in clause 2 is inclusive of all maintenance charges for the said premises, and no separate maintenance charge shall be demanded by the Landlord during the subsistence of this agreement.

4. The Tenant has paid to the Landlord a refundable security deposit of Rs. 42,000/- (Rupees Forty Two Thousand only), to be refunded at the time of handing over vacant possession after adjustment of any arrears of rent or the cost of repairing damage caused by the Tenant.

5. Electricity and water shall be charged to the Tenant as per the meter readings and the bills raised by the supplying authorities.

6. The Tenant shall use the said premises for residential purposes only and shall not sublet, assign or part with possession of the whole or any part of the said premises.

7. The Tenant shall not carry out any structural alteration to the said premises without the prior written consent of the Landlord.

8. The Landlord shall be responsible for structural repairs to the said premises, and the Tenant shall be responsible for day-to-day minor repairs.

9. Either party may terminate this agreement by giving to the other one month's notice in writing. Neither party shall be entitled to terminate this agreement on shorter notice.

10. On the expiry or earlier termination of this agreement, the Tenant shall hand over vacant and peaceful possession of the said premises to the Landlord.

IN WITNESS WHEREOF the parties have set their hands on the day and year first written above.

Landlord: Sunil Bharti

Tenant: Rehana Qureshi
